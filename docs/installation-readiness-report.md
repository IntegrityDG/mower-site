**3. Accounting, processor and scheduling behavior**

Cash receipts remain immutable. A $750 entry for $75 actually received produces a linked $675 recording correction, leaves charges unchanged and does not claim a refund. An actual cash return requires IDS to confirm that money was handed back outside Stripe. It links to the original receipt and is capped against its remaining eligible cash after corrections and completed returns. The operation records evidence, financial state and audit in one transaction. Matching retries return the prior result; conflicting keys, concurrent over-returns and stale balance arguments are rejected. Explicit overpayment consent is required.

The shared integer-cent calculator preserves all requested examples: $1,000 charges minus $250 deposit leaves $750; another $750 cash settles it; another $50 leaves $700; a completed $50 refund from $250 leaves $200 net paid; reducing $1,000 charges to $940 leaves $60 credit until a completed $60 refund clears it. Price credits, recording corrections, pending refunds and completed returns have distinct effects. Historical saved balances are labeled separately from refreshed current balances, and saved-operation confirmation survives a refresh failure.

Stripe reconciliation validates ownership, stored payment and purpose, Session/PaymentIntent/Charge references, successful captured evidence, amount/currency, metadata and TEST mode before crediting money. Durable reservations store stable keys and the exact external request before network calls; no database locks are held over those calls. Recovery reuses or locates the same processor object after persistence failure. Expired/superseded sessions can be replaced safely. Unresolved old reservations stop for review instead of silently issuing another operation after the safe retry window. Payment, financial state, event and audit writes are atomic in PostgreSQL.

Refunds retain processor references and lifecycle states. Requested/pending is not money returned; failed/canceled refunds do not reduce net paid. Older pending events cannot overwrite confirmed success. Original receipt credit survives partial refunds. Occurrence evidence is stored separately from notification arrival/processing; missing success-occurrence evidence remains unknown rather than being fabricated from notification time. An old deposit event cannot downgrade full settlement, including an inside-72-hour required payment whose historical purpose is `deposit`. Cash received during an open online checkout and a later successful checkout both remain recorded as credit; neither triggers an automatic refund.

Server and database validation enforce future four-hour appointments, horizon, configured Chicago working windows/start grid, blackouts, raw occupancy and approval/reschedule revalidation. Installations may be adjacent when their actual ranges do not overlap; the established demo/demo one-hour exclusion remains. Two actual writers tested demo-first and installation-first conflicts. Explicit Chicago spring-gap and fall-fold cases are covered; the existing earlier occurrence of a repeated local hour is used consistently.

Locked rules remain installation-specific: $800 labor plus $200 actual-use materials allowance; $250 credited deposit; ordinary balance before the 72-hour boundary; inside-72-hour full initial requirement unless IDS approves an arrangement; cash does not authorize unpaid work. Cash failure permits one reschedule, followed by the normal deadline and entire $250 deposit forfeiture if missed. Unused labor and actual materials are reconciled distinctly. Extra labor is $125/hour in begun 15-minute increments after four cumulative work hours. New work sessions preserve exact elapsed seconds; billing increments apply to the accumulated total, avoiding per-pause minute inflation. Corrections supersede rather than double-count prior time. Legacy sessions retain their recorded minute precision. Travel includes 120 minutes each direction, then $35 per begun excess hour per direction: 150 minutes each way is $70. Underground labor is $50 per started 10 feet, with applicable materials; grounding labor is included and materials separate. There is no additional customer-caused return-trip fee. Approved pricing snapshots do not change with future defaults.

Cancellation, placement refusal, safety/termination, remediation, weather, lodging, utility marking, connectivity and documentation terms were retained. The released existing-order refund-dispatch fix, demo dispatch, services-top navigation, refund disclosure and equipment-checkout installation-request selection remain covered by the source tests. No installation charge was added to machine checkout. The unrelated mobile Back-scroll investigation remains deferred.
