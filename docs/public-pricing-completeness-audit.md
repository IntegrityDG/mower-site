## Policy Findings

- Remaining universal rule: every active physical product must have a visible numeric price, and every fixed-price service must have a visible numeric price.
- Approved exception: `service-repair-visit` and all connected product-service offerings are approved variable-price services and must display `Please call for pricing` instead of a fixed numeric price.
- Customer-facing Service Repair Visit explanation: ?Repair pricing is determined by warranty coverage, travel, diagnostics, labor, and required parts.?
- Service Repair Visit CTA: use a button labeled `Call for Service Pricing` routed to the configured IDS business phone number. Do not hardcode a phone number when a site-wide contact value or configuration exists.
- No active physical product, variant, option, or package currently lacks a visible public price.
- Make an Offer should be limited to visible-priced physical sellable rows only: products, variants, standalone options/accessories/modules, and packages. It should not appear on service rows, service payment options, product-service offerings, financing, taxes, travel, maintenance plans, repair visits, or other non-physical services.
- Included or zero-dollar configuration choices are physical records with visible $0.00 pricing, but they should not receive Make an Offer controls unless IDS reclassifies them as standalone merchandise.
- IDS has approved standard monthly prices for the three fixed-price service plans: Essential Care at $79/month, Performance Management at $99/month, and Full Property Management at $149/month. The standard mowing season is nine months.
- Existing service payment options already contain the approved monthly and 9-month season-prepay amounts for those three plans. Preserve those payment-option prices and savings labels; do not invent annual, seasonal, or extra discounts.
- Current service-side fixed pricing remains incomplete in the database until the three base `catalog_services` rows receive their approved monthly `regular_price_cents` values. The related product-service offerings can inherit those base prices through the existing catalog API and should not duplicate service prices unless IDS later approves a product-specific override.
- The current frontend still contains a generic `Contact for pricing` fallback in `lib/catalog/pricing.ts` and a few summary surfaces. That fallback must be replaced with numeric-price handling for fixed-price items and the approved Service Repair Visit call-pricing display only.
- Offer submissions must resolve the current public price server-side from active visible physical catalog records only. Service records, repair visits, hidden/inactive records, and unpriced physical records must be rejected.
