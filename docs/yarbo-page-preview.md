Individual Yarbo Equipment can be purchased by existing Yarbo owners, customers assembling a custom system manually, or customers adding another seasonal capability. Modules do not operate by themselves.

## 5. Core-Required Notices

Place this notice:

- on every module-only card
- in the module detail drawer or product section
- in the request/cart summary for individual module order lines
- near quantity controls

Exact notice:

Module only — requires a Yarbo Core to operate.

Do not require the customer to identify as a new owner or existing owner before seeing individual equipment.

## 6. Included Charging And RTK Equipment Explanation

Customer-facing copy:

Complete Yarbo packages include Yarbo Core and the Core equipment needed for the platform foundation, including charging and docking equipment, battery equipment, Data Center/navigation equipment, and installation/tool equipment where included by the official Core package.

Do not list Core charging, battery, power, cable, mounting, RTK, or navigation equipment as extra optional package selections.

## 7. Warranty And Ownership Guidance

Warranty copy:

Official Yarbo pages reviewed in this phase consistently show 2-Year Warranty, 30-Day Hassle-Free Returns, and 24/7 Support. IDS should verify dealer-specific handling before final publication.

Ownership guidance:

Choose a Complete Yarbo Package when you want Core and one or more modules together. Choose Individual Yarbo Equipment when you already have Core, want a replacement/base purchase, or want to assemble a custom system manually. Module-only equipment requires a Yarbo Core to operate.
